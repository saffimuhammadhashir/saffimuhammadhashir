# Saffi Muhammad Hashir | Co-founder & CTO at Chrio | Co-founder of Repic | Real-time computer vision and production AI

I co-founded Chrio, where I lead engineering on real-time computer vision for cricket broadcasts, and Repic, an AI brand studio that turns an idea into finished, on-brand content. I like taking research-grade models and turning them into fast, reliable products that people actually use.

## What I build

- **[Chrio](https://chrio.site)**: cricket broadcast AI, with real-time detection and tracking on live match video.
- **[Dhundo](https://dhundo.store)**: product search that prices one query across 136 Pakistani online stores and ranks the results.
- **[Repic](https://repic.site)**: an AI brand studio that turns one brand profile into a brand kit, posts, carousels and stories, with consent-gated AI Twins.
